Catchment areas: nearest by distance or by travel time

    LC 01 · observe

    With all five facilities open and straight-line (Voronoi) assignment, which facility serves the most people?

    Read "busiest facility" and the load bars at baseline.
    Williamsburg serves 278,696 people. Its straight-line catchment reaches west over the East River into Manhattan, because a Voronoi cell ignores the water.

    LC 02 · intervene

    Switch the rule to travel time. How many block groups change facility?

    Count the outlined block groups after you switch, or read "block groups that switch".
    31 of 531 block groups (53,333 people) switch, most of them Manhattan blocks that Williamsburg claimed across the river. The busiest facility becomes Union Square with 277,541 people.

    LC 03 · transfer

    A planner closes one facility. Which rule would you trust to estimate the new loads, and why?

    Close City Hall, then compare the busiest load under each rule.
    With City Hall closed, Union Square's load is 294,837 by straight line but 329,510 by travel time: the straight-line rule sends some of City Hall's people across the river. Trust the travel-time rule; the error lands exactly on the facility that must absorb the closure.
