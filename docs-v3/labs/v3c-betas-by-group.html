LC 01 · observe

At baseline, what is the pooled slope, and how many municipalities have a slope above it?

Read "pooled slope" and "slopes above pooled" in the sidebar; the blue line in the histogram is the pooled slope.
The pooled slope is 0.0845 (solar rate per month); 62 of 146 municipalities sit above it. The pooled line is not the median of the betas: a few steep municipalities pull it, so fewer than half beat it. 0.0172 is the highlighted municipality's own slope.

LC 02 · intervene

Raise the minimum months per group to 30. What happens to the spread of slopes, and why?

Watch "SD of slopes" and "groups fitted" as the histogram rebuilds. Which municipalities can estimate a slope from only a handful of months?
Groups fitted fall from 146 to 75 and the SD of slopes falls from 0.0947 to 0.0607. Some of the spread came from groups with few months, whose slopes are imprecise; the rest is real variation between municipalities that survives the filter. The pooled slope also moves (0.0845 to 0.0930).

LC 03 · transfer

When would a single pooled beta mislead a decision-maker comparing municipalities?

Switch the view to "Pooled only" and compare it with the per-group lines of the highlighted municipality.
A pooled beta can be precise and still wrong for every unit you care about. Here it says 0.0845 per month, yet the highlighted municipality grows at 0.0172 and the betas spread with an SD of 0.0947. A decision about specific municipalities needs the per-group betas.
