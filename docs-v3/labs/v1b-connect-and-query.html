    LC 02 · intervene
    Move collect() to the first line. What happens to rows transferred and to the SQL sent?
    
      
    
      
    Press "First line" and compare the SQL under the diagram with the baseline SQL.
    
      
    The result is still 4 rows, but 336,776 rows × 19 columns cross the wire (about 41 seconds at 10 Mbps, against under a millisecond). The SQL loses its WHERE and GROUP BY: R does that work after the download.
    
    
    LC 03 · transfer
    Why does lazy evaluation let a 336,776-row table answer a question in milliseconds from a laptop?
    
      
    
      
    Step through the life cycle: which step is the first one that moves rows?
