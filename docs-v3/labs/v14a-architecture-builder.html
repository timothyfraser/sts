    LC 01 · observe

    In the default design for the air-quality brief (React sums the raw hourly rows), which requirement fails?

    Read the red light under Requirements, then the payload estimate.
    Payload fails: about 3,943 KB of raw readings go to the browser against a 250 KB limit. Freshness (60 min against 120) and cost ($35.15 against $40) pass.

    LC 02 · intervene

    Move the aggregation into a materialised view refreshed hourly by pg_cron. Which requirement flips, and what does it cost?

    Watch the payload light and the delta next to monthly cost.
    The view ships 1,576 daily means (98.5 KB), so payload passes. The source only updates hourly, so staleness stays 60 min. Hourly refreshes add cron compute but cut egress: $35.15 becomes $38.88.

    LC 03 · transfer

    Your stakeholder now needs per-person data. What must change in the architecture, and why?

    Set Row grain to per person and watch the privacy light, then try RLS on.
    Per-person rows fail privacy until row level security is on: the policy person_id = auth.uid() limits each reader to their own rows, and the light turns back to pass.
