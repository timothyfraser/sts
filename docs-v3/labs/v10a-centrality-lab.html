    LC 01 · observe

    At baseline (unweighted betweenness), which station has the highest betweenness?

    Read "top station" in the sidebar, or the first bar in the ranking. The biggest circle on the map is the same station.
    D32016, with betweenness of about 42.3: it lies on more shortest paths between other stations than any other. D32024 has the most rides (strength 2,124) but comes second on betweenness.

    LC 02 · intervene

    Remove D32016. Which station's betweenness rises most, and what happens to the average path length?

    Watch "biggest rise" and "avg path (hops)" in the sidebar; the blue bar marks the station that picked up the most detours.
    The 18 edges through D32016 disappear, so trips that passed through it reroute. B32010 picks up the most (about +13.1 betweenness), D32024 becomes the new top (about 36.7), and the mean shortest path rises from 1.68 to about 1.76 hops.

    LC 03 · transfer

    Why can a station with modest degree still have high betweenness, and what does that mean for a bike-share operator?

    Compare the Degree and Betweenness rankings: the order changes, because degree counts neighbours while betweenness counts the paths a station sits on.
    Degree counts links; betweenness counts the shortest paths between other stations that run through a station. A bridge between clusters can have few links and still carry many paths. Removing D32016 here pushed about 13 extra units of betweenness onto B32010 and lengthened trips, which is what an operator sees when a bridge station is empty or closed.
