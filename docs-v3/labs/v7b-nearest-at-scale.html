LC 01 · observe
At 1× with brute force, how many distance comparisons does the query make?

      

      
Read "comparisons" in the sidebar. Brute force does not know which polls are close until it has measured them all.

      
65,025 = 255 origins × 255 polls. The 620 pairs within 500 m are only known after all 65,025 distances are measured.

    
LC 02 · intervene
Choose Index, turn the index on, and set the scale to 100×. How many candidate comparisons remain, and what changes in the plan?

      

      
Compare the two bars and the EXPLAIN box before and after you press "Index on".

      
With the GiST index the engine only visits the 62,000 pairs whose boxes overlap the 500 m radius, instead of 6,502,500 (25,500 × 255). The plan line changes from "Seq Scan on polls" to "Index Scan using polls_geom_idx on polls".

    
LC 03 · transfer
Why can a pre-filter with ST_DWithin miss the true nearest facility, and how do you guard against it?

      

      
Set the threshold to 250 m and count the dashed lines, then read "missed".

      
A radius is a filter, not a search: at 250 m, 164 of 255 precincts have no poll inside it, and even at 500 m 51 are missed, though every one has a nearest poll (mean 379 m away). The KNN operator <-> with LIMIT 1 walks the index outward until it finds one, so it never comes back empty; with a fixed radius, count the misses and widen it.
