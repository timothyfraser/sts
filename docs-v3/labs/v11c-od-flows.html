    Learning checks

    LC 01 · observe

    At the default settings (county zones, gross flows, minimum 100), what share of the total county-to-county flow is kept?

    Read the "Flow retained" bar or the "share kept" metric. Top N only changes what is drawn, not what is kept.
    98.7%: 343 county pairs carry 504,642 of the 511,414 extra trips. Small pairs are numerous but light, so a threshold of 100 drops many rows and very little flow. (81% is what a threshold of 1,000 keeps.)

    LC 02 · intervene

    Switch Direction from Gross to Net at county level. What happens to the biggest gross corridor, Miami-Dade to Broward?

    Net flow is flow minus the reverse corridor. Watch the top corridor in the status bar and the flow kept metric.
    Miami-Dade to Broward is 23,208 and Broward to Miami-Dade is 22,954, so the net is 254 and it drops out of the top corridors. Net flow kept falls from 504,642 to 26,599 and the top corridor becomes Houston to Henry County in Alabama. At state level the top corridor also changes, from FL to GA (2,645 gross) to GA to AL (net 168).

    LC 03 · transfer

    Why can aggregating to larger zones hide the evacuation routes that matter most?

    Switch Zone level to State and compare flow kept with the county total. Look at the filter(origin_zone != dest_zone) line.
    Most Dorian evacuation ran inside Florida (Miami-Dade, Broward, Brevard, Volusia). At state level those trips have the same origin and destination zone, so the OD table keeps only 6,468 of the 511,414 extra trips and the busiest routes disappear.
