LC 01 · observe

At baseline, how many hours are kept?

Read "hours kept" in the sidebar. The page holds a sample, not the whole file.
240: every sampled hour is at or above 0 °F, so the filter keeps them all.

LC 02 · intervene

Set the minimum to 70 °F. What happens to the mean temperature of the kept hours?

Watch the delta next to "mean temp".
A filter that drops the cold hours raises the mean of what remains, from about 55 °F to about 79 °F.

Toggle the sort here and watch the metrics.
