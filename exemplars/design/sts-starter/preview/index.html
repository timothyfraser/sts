Controls

Three button roles and one chip. Primary is the one thing to do on a screen. Info reveals or explains. Secondary does everything else.

Rules the template enforces

TOKENSComponents use var(--token) only. A raw hex value in a component is a lint error.
SPACINGSeven steps, 4 to 48 px. Gaps between siblings come from gap, not margins.
RADIUSOne radius, 8 px. Pills are for chips only.
NUMBERSTabular numerals in every table, KPI and axis.
STATESLoading, empty and error are built in every fetch hook and rendered by every panel.
PHONEOne column at 400 px; no horizontal scroll; 44 px touch targets.
DARKBoth themes from the same tokens; the accent is legible on both grounds.
